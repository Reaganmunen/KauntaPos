Import products

Upload a CSV file (save one from Excel or Google Sheets). The first row names the columns. Only name is required. Optional: selling_price, cost_price, opening_quantity, sku, barcode, category, brand, unit, reorder_level and tax_code. For loose items add sold_by_weight (yes or no), unit (for example kg) and scale_plu. For a carton barcode add carton_barcode and carton_quantity (units in the carton).
