Start with the till. Switch on the rest when you are ready.

Offline selling

The line drops and the queue does not. Sales are saved on the device and sent as soon as you are back online, in the order they were rung up.

KRA eTIMS receipts

Each sale is signed and reported to KRA, so the receipt your customer holds is the one the taxman sees.

M-Pesa STK push

Prompt the customer's phone from the till and watch it confirm.

Roles and PIN login

Cashiers switch with a PIN. Managers see the numbers cashiers can't.

Shifts

Branches

Stock, staff and reports for each location, rolled up for you.

Expenses

Record rent, transport and wages so profit means profit.

Audit trail

Who changed a price, voided a sale or adjusted stock.
